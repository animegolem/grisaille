## The project in four sentences

The gradient of cross-entropy on a refusal target is the Rescorla–Wagner
delta rule, so refusal training is extinction learning in the formal
sense and inherits Bouton's relapse taxonomy (renewal, drift,
reacquisition). AI safety has rediscovered each effect under its own
name and cites neither Bouton nor Craske. Toy models (disc1–disc3, 36
cells) already show: safety training becomes context-bound only when
context participates in the task, dose-dependent; mapping-type targets
renew to the original, constant refusal leaks to noise; nothing is
erased (savings 36/36); the constructive arm is most drift-resistant at
unmatched budget. The paper then tests the alternative (constructive /
reactivation-conditioned training with affect latents clamped) on
Gemma 3 with the Jacobian lens open.
